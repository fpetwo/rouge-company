# Auras for a summoner army

<p class="lead">Auras are the biggest damage multiplier for skeletons, but each one needs a carrier and a free slot. This page lists every aura source that matters for a summoner and shows who can wear it.</p>

## The four carriers

- **Necromancer**: one weapon slot (two-handed weapons cost the shield), armor, rings. Can swap weapons after summoning without weakening the existing army.
- **Rogue (Act 1 merc)**: bows and crossbows only, plus armor and helm. Brings Inner Sight.
- **Desert merc (Act 2)**: polearms and spears only, plus armor and helm. The Offensive merc brings his own Might aura, which grows with +skills on his gear.
- **Iron Golem**: built from one metal item. It takes over the item's aura, but no bows or crossbows. The item is lost when the golem dies.

You hire either the Rogue or the Act 2 merc, never both, and you have one Iron Golem.

## Stacking rules

- **Item aura levels never rise with +skills.** Only the Act 2 merc's own aura does.
- **Same aura, same carrier: levels add up.** Edge and Bramble on one Rogue give Thorns 36.
- **Same aura, different carriers: only the highest counts.** Last Wish on the Necromancer and an Act 2 Might merc do not stack.

## Radius matters

The calculator assumes every minion is inside every aura. In practice Fanaticism has the smallest radius (about 17 yards at level 15), so it works best on a carrier that stays in the middle of the army. Concentration, Might and Thorns cover 30–37 yards.

## Combinations that work

| Setup | Necromancer | Merc | Iron Golem |
|---|---|---|---|
| Four damage auras | Last Wish (after summoning) | Rogue: Faith + Bramble | Pride |
| Act 2 Might | – | Act 2: own Might, Pride, Bramble | Beast or Mania |
| Rogue classic | – | Rogue: Mist + Bramble | Beast or Mania |

Conviction (Infinity) only fits if a polearm slot is free, which usually costs either Pride or the Last Wish setup. For physical skeletons Conviction only helps through lower defense; its resistance reduction helps mages and elemental revives.
