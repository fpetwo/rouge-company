# Hell monster data

<p class="lead">The monsters available in the calculator, both as enemies and as revives. Values are Hell difficulty at each monster's native level.</p>

{{table:monsters}}

## As enemies

- **Physical resistance** decides how hard your skeletons hit. Death Lords (66%) take a third of the damage a Flesh Archer (15%) takes without Amplify Damage. With Amplify Damage active, every monster loses 100% physical resistance.
- **Elemental immunities** only matter for mages and elemental revives. Flesh Archers are immune to cold, Doom Knights and Venom Lords to fire.
- **Melee enemies feed Thorns.** A pack of Doom Knights hitting your skeletons damages itself; ranged archers never do.

## As revives

A revive keeps the monster's own level, attack rating and damage, and your auras boost its physical damage like any other minion. Melee monsters with high base damage and attack rating, such as Doom Knights and Venom Lords, profit most. Ranged revives stay behind the army but have lower base damage.

## Data source

Stats from the [Diablo Wiki](https://diablo2.diablowiki.net/) monster pages, which document Lord of Destruction 1.11 values. Monsters in areas with a higher area level spawn stronger, and D2R balance changes after 1.11 are not reflected. Monster attack speed is not part of this data; the calculator uses an editable assumption instead.
