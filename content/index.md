# D2R Summoner DPS Calculator

## How to use the calculator

1. **Pick a preset** as a starting point: Low budget, Rogue Company, Desert Storm or Max Auras. Each one is a complete, wearable gear setup. Low budget, Rogue Company and Desert Storm also offer Beast mode for the Iron Golem.
2. **Enter your skill levels** including all +skills. Use the "during play" column if you summon with one set of gear and fight with another, for example the Last Wish swap.
3. **Tick the auras** you have and set their levels. The aura panel tells you immediately whether the combination fits into your gear slots.
4. **Choose revives and an enemy pack** from real Hell monsters. The army's damage is shared across the pack, and Thorns counts every melee attacker.
5. **Copy the link** to share your exact setup. Everything is stored in the address bar, nothing is saved on a server.

## What the numbers mean

The result is the combined damage per second of skeleton warriors, skeletal mages, the Iron Golem, revives and Thorns. It assumes every minion stands inside every aura radius and keeps attacking. In real fights the army spreads out and spends time walking, so treat the numbers as a fair comparison between setups, not as a time-to-kill prediction. The [model guide](/guide/how-it-works/) lists every formula and assumption, the [aura guide](/guide/auras/) shows which item can go on which carrier, and the [presets guide](/guide/presets/) compares the four setups side by side.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which auras increase skeleton damage the most?

Concentration (Pride, Mist), Might (Last Wish or an Act 2 Offensive merc) and Fanaticism (Faith, Beast, Mania) all add percentage damage for every minion in range. Fanaticism also adds attack speed and attack rating. They add up, but each one needs its own carrier slot.

### Can I run Might, Concentration, Fanaticism and Thorns at the same time?

Yes, with Last Wish on the Necromancer (after summoning), Pride in the Iron Golem and Faith plus Bramble on the Rogue. Conviction does not fit on top, because Infinity needs a polearm slot that is already taken.

### Do skeletons keep their stats when I swap gear?

Yes. Damage, attack rating and mage missile level are fixed when the minion is summoned. Only the maximum number follows your current skill level, so minions above the new limit disappear after a swap.

### Rogue or Act 2 merc for a summoner?

The Rogue brings Inner Sight and can carry Faith or Mist. The Act 2 Offensive merc brings his own Might aura and can carry Pride or Infinity. Which is stronger depends on the rest of your gear; the presets in the calculator compare both.

### Why is the damage lower against real monsters than against the model monster?

Real Hell monsters have physical resistance (Doom Knights 33%, Death Lords 66%) and elemental immunities that the model monster does not have. Amplify Damage removes 100% physical resistance, which is why it matters so much for skeletons.
