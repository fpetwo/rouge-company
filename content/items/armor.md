# Armor bases

<p class="lead">{{count:armor}} armor bases, grouped by slot and class. Within each type, every row block is one item family: the normal base followed by its exceptional and elite versions.</p>

Defense is the range a white base can roll. Ethereal items get 50% more base defense and a 10 lower Str requirement. Block is the base chance to block before class and level adjustments. See [how to read the tables](/items/#how-to-read-the-tables) for tiers and sockets.
