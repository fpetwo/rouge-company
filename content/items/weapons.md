# Weapon bases

<p class="lead">{{count:weapons}} weapon bases, grouped by type. Within each type, every row block is one item family: the normal base followed by its exceptional and elite versions.</p>

Damage is the white base damage before any modifiers. Two-hand damage applies to two-handed weapons; swords that show both columns can be used one-handed by a Barbarian. Ethereal items have 50% more base damage and 10 lower Str and Dex requirements. See [how to read the tables](/items/#how-to-read-the-tables) for tiers, speed and sockets.
