# Privacy

<p class="lead">This is a free, non-commercial fan project. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, shows no ads, loads no third-party resources and has no user accounts or forms.</p>

## Hosting

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. When you visit the site, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, whether or not you are signed in to GitHub. The site itself does not store or evaluate this data. Details: [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) and the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Calculator settings

Your calculator settings are stored only in the address bar (the part after `#`) so you can share a setup as a link. This part of the address is never sent to the server.
